steer with the joystick or the cursor keys, Esc to quit – this one runs on a plain 1040 ST without blitter: the 68000 drips the mountains down, draws the sun, the planets and all the stars itself and changes the colours in the vertical blank – no STE needed, but it does not hurt – the planets are made from a seed while you fly and the palettes are mixed on the fly too – every change of the autopilot brings something new – greetings to everybody who still loves Rescue on Fractalus – BTW you can always leave the autopilot and fly around yourself!
