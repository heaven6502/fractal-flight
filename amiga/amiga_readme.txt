steer with the joystick or the cursor keys, Esc to quit – this one runs on a plain A500: the blitter drips the mountains down, the copper fades the colours and hands out the planes, the sun and all the stars are hardware sprites – no fast RAM needed, but a 68030 does not hurt – the planets are made from a seed while you fly and the palettes are mixed on the fly too – every change of the autopilot brings something new – greetings to everybody who still loves Rescue on Fractalus – BTW you can always leave the autopilot and fly around yourself!
